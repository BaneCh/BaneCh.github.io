About Me

I'm a Civil & Traffic Engineer with over a decade of experience across the UK, having worked with organisations including Transport for London, WSP, Amey, and various local authorities. In 2025, I founded Traffiq to modernise how traffic data is collected, analysed, and transformed into actionable insights — combining deep field survey expertise with a practical approach to provide engineers, local authorities, and organisations with the reliable data they need for their traffic studies, designs, and policy decisions.

While my roots are firmly in civil engineering, I'm genuinely curious about technology and what the new era of AI and digital tools can bring to the industry.
